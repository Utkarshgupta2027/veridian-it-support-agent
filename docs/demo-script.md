# Demo

1. Laptop dead at 3.5 years: route to IT + Finance & Assets for verification and policy review; do not invent approval.
2. Guest Wi-Fi: resolve through the front-desk kiosk; no IT ticket is created.
3. Password lockout: resolve through manual IT unlock after 5 failed attempts; no approval is required.
4. Non-catalog software: route to Security review; explain the 3-5 business day review.
5. VPN expired: explain 90-day renewal and employee renewal responsibility.
6. Printer paper jam: show queue -> spooler -> asset-tag ticket next action.
7. Home equipment: route for manager sign-off and Finance processing before IT shipping.
8. Phishing: escalate to Security, show security@veridian-corp.example, and explicitly prohibit forwarding.
9. Mailbox full: show 25GB default, archive guidance, manager approval above 25GB, and 50GB maximum.
10. Admin access: ask for business justification and show relevant TK-1050 history without inventing permission rules.
11. Ambiguous request: ask for the service/device, error, and expected behavior.

Show the decision, reason, Knowledge Source, Department, Ticket ID when applicable, Next Action, history context, and audit trail after each request.
